Shades & Tints

Preview how a color looks lighter (tints) and darker (shades). A quick way to build a tonal scale.

Open the free Shades & Tints →

Shades & Tints shows your base color blended step by step toward white (tints) and toward black (shades).

Use it to build hover states, borders, and soft backgrounds that stay on-palette.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a shade and a tint?

A tint mixes the color toward white; a shade mixes it toward black.

How many steps are shown?

Six tints and six shades, evenly spaced from the base color.

Are the values exact?

They are computed by linear RGB interpolation and rounded to the nearest HEX.
