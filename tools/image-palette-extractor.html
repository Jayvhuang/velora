The Image Palette Extractor reads an image you choose and finds its dominant colors using on-device analysis.

Your image is never uploaded. Analysis happens locally, so it stays private by design.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. The image is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to a server.

How many colors are extracted?

Up to six dominant colors, approximated from a downscaled copy of the image.

Is the result exact?

It is an approximation of the most common colors and is meant as a starting palette.
