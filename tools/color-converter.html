Velora's Color Converter turns one color value into every common format at once. Type or pick a color and HEX, RGB, HSL, and HSV update together.

Frequently asked questions

What formats does the Color Converter support?

HEX, RGB, HSL, and HSV. Editing any one updates the others in real time.

Is the Color Converter free?

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser and needs no account or upload.

How accurate are the conversions?

Values use standard sRGB-to-HSL/HSV formulas, matching common design tools to within rounding.
