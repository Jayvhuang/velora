Contrast Checker

Check if a text and background color pair meets WCAG 2.1 AA and AAA accessibility standards. Free and instant.

Open the free Contrast Checker →

The Contrast Checker measures the contrast ratio between a text color and a background color and reports pass/fail against WCAG 2.1 thresholds for normal and large text.

Use it to make sure buttons, body text, and headings stay readable for everyone, including users with low vision.

Frequently asked questions

What contrast ratio is needed for WCAG AA?

A ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text.

How is the contrast ratio calculated?

With the official WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula, the same method referenced in accessibility guidelines.

Does it check AAA as well?

Yes. It reports AAA for both normal text (7:1) and large text (4.5:1).
