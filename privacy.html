What we collect

我们收集什么

Velora is a set of browser-based tools. Your colors, HEX values, and uploaded images are processed entirely on your device. We do not receive, store, or transmit your colors or images to any server.

Velora 是一组基于浏览器的工具。你的颜色、HEX 值以及上传的图片 完全在你的设备上处理。我们不会接收、存储或将你的颜色或图片传输到任何服务器。

We do not require an account and do not knowingly collect personal information such as your name, email, or location through the tools themselves.

我们无需账号，也不会通过工具本身有意收集你的姓名、邮箱或位置等个人信息。

Cookies and advertising

Cookie 与广告

Velora may display Google AdSense advertisements. AdSense and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Velora 可能会展示 Google AdSense 广告。AdSense 及其合作伙伴可能会使用 Cookie，根据你对本站或其他网站的访问记录投放广告。你可以通过 Google 广告设置 选择退出个性化广告。

Affiliate links

联盟链接

Velora participates in the Amazon Associates program. Some links to Amazon are affiliate links, and we may earn a commission from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you.

Velora 参与亚马逊联盟（Amazon Associates）计划。部分指向亚马逊的链接为联盟链接，我们可能从合格购买中获得佣金，这不会增加你的支出。

Children's privacy

儿童隐私

Velora is a general-purpose educational toolkit and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Velora 是面向大众的教科级工具箱，并非面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。
